# Ivan's Standard German Rate Card (2025)

All prices are for labor only, excluding materials and VAT, unless noted otherwise. Use the midpoint of a range as the default price unless Ivan specifies a different rate for a job. Region: Germany generally; the painting-prep table is specific to Hessen. VAT (MwSt) is 19% unless Ivan says otherwise.

## General construction works — Germany 2025

### Drywall (Гіпсокартон)
- Drywall wall on metal frame: 35–50 €/m²
- Drywall ceiling, single-level: 40–60 €/m²
- Drywall partition with soundproofing: 50–70 €/m²

### Filling / skim coating (Шпаклівка)
- Joint filling (Q1–Q2), no finish leveling: 5–8 €/m²
- Wall skim coat (Q3–Q4), paint-ready: 10–18 €/m²

### Painting (Фарбування)
- Wall painting, 2 coats: 8–12 €/m²
- Ceiling painting, 2 coats: 10–15 €/m²

### Flooring (Підлога)
- Laminate installation, with underlay: 10–15 €/m²
- Vinyl flooring installation, click system: 12–18 €/m²
- Linoleum installation, glued: 10–14 €/m²

### Wallpaper (Шпалери)
- Wallpaper hanging (fleece/vinyl): 10–16 €/m²
- Old wallpaper removal, with wall cleaning: 3–6 €/m²

### Floor demolition (Демонтаж підлоги)
- Removal of laminate / vinyl / linoleum, incl. waste removal: 5–10 €/m²

## Painting preparation — Hessen 2025 (labor only)
- Window masking: 2–4 €/piece or 1–2 €/linear meter
- Door masking (frame, hardware, vents): 3–6 €/piece
- Baseboard / floor-edge masking: 1–3 €/linear meter
- Floor covering with film/paper: 1.5–3 €/m²
- Full painting prep package (masking + film + removal): 3–5 €/m²

- These are Ivan's own historical price references (docs: "Ціни_будівельні_роботи_Німеччина_2025" and "Гессен_Підготовка_до_фарбування_ціни_робота" in his Drive). Treat them as defaults, not hard rules — always let an explicit price from Ivan override the table.
